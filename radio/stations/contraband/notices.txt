Read by LibriVox volunteers, who give their recordings to the public domain. The books play in order, a chapter at a time, round the clock.

Milton printed Areopagitica in 1644 without the licence it argued against. Common Sense came out in 1776 with no author's name on it. Candide was condemned in Geneva and Paris the year it appeared.

Douglass sailed for Britain in 1845, months after his Narrative named the men who had owned him. Thoreau spent a night in the Concord jail for refusing his poll tax. Emma Goldman was deported from the United States in 1919.
