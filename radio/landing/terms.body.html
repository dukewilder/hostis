Pyrate Radio · HOSTIS's position

Pyrate Radio is free software, released under CC0. HOSTIS writes it and gives it away, and asks that it be used only within the law.

What You Air

yours to answer for

Pyrate Radio is for lawful broadcasting. Air what you made, what is in the public domain where you and your listeners are, or what its makers have freed under a licence that lets anyone broadcast it. Air nothing that is illegal where you are, and no one else's copyrighted work without their permission. HOSTIS condones no unlawful use of the radio.

Your Station

HOSTIS does not run it

Every station belongs to whoever holds its key, and that keeper alone chooses what it plays and answers for it. HOSTIS holds no accounts, and it neither hosts, reviews, ranks, approves, runs nor profits from anyone else's station.

HOSTIS's Own Machines

its Lanterns and its stations

HOSTIS runs Lanterns. On those machines it can stop carrying a station, and will when it receives a valid legal notice about that station. That reaches only HOSTIS's machines.

HOSTIS raises and uploads only its own stations, and they air only what is in the public domain or free for anyone to broadcast. Every recording on them, with its licence and its source, is listed under Sources.

Copyright Notices

for anything on HOSTIS's machines

Send notices about anything carried on HOSTIS's own machines to hostisblack@pm.me. Say what work you hold rights in and which station carries it, by its id or its link.

No Promises

as it is

The software comes as it is, with no warranty. How it works sets out what it protects against and what it cannot. Read the Limits before you rely on it.
