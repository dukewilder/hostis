What You Air

yours to answer for

Air only what you made, what is in the public domain where you and your listeners are, or what its makers have freed under a licence that lets anyone broadcast it. Do not air anyone else's copyrighted work without their permission. Do not air anything that is illegal where you are.

Your Station

HOSTIS does not run it

Every station belongs to whoever holds its key. HOSTIS does not host, review, rank or approve stations, and holds no account for you.

HOSTIS's Own Machines

a Lantern and relays

HOSTIS runs a Lantern and relays. On those machines it can stop carrying a station, and will when it receives a valid legal notice about that station. That reaches only HOSTIS's machines.

HOSTIS's own stations air only what is in the public domain or free for anyone to broadcast. Every recording on them, with its licence and its source, is listed under Sources.
