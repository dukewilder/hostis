Your Colours

kept in this browser

What reaches you is your own choice. A marooned station leaves this browser's Log. Lists of marooned stations pass from hand to hand: paste one in to take it aboard, or copy yours out to share.

Each station says for itself whether it is mature or explicit. A station that lies about it is yours to maroon.

What this browser keeps

Your switches and marooned stations, the harbours it has met, your name and mutes in the chatter, the secret each room's voice is drawn from, and any pocket invitations you opened. It sends none of this anywhere. The address bar holds the public station you are on, and a pocket's never.

Forgetting it all makes this browser a stranger to the radio: a new voice in every room, and no pockets.
