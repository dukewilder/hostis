Write these down

These twenty-four words are the station. Whoever holds them can speak for it, strike it or take it to another device. They are shown once, never sent anywhere and never kept here. Write them on paper, in order. Nobody from Pyrate Radio or HOSTIS will ever ask for them.

    Your Colours

    kept in this browser

    What reaches you is your own choice. A marooned station leaves this browser's Log. Lists of marooned stations pass from hand to hand: paste one in to take it aboard, or copy yours out to share.

    Each station says for itself whether it is mature or explicit. A station that lies about it is yours to maroon.

    What this browser keeps

    Your switches and marooned stations, the harbours it has met, your name and mutes in the chatter, the secret each room's voice is drawn from, any pocket invitations you opened, and the deputy key of any station you raised here (never its twenty-four words). It sends none of this anywhere. The address bar holds the public station you are on, and a pocket's never.

    Forgetting it all makes this browser a stranger to the radio: a new voice in every room, and no pockets.
