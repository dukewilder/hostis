    How private is broadcasting from a browser?

    Your station passes through other people's radios before it reaches anyone, so no listener can trace it to this tab. The relay this page connects through can see your address, though never what you send. Open this page in Tor Browser to hide your address from the relay too. The app on a phone is more private still.

        What this browser keeps

        Your settings, kept and hidden stations, the list of stations last on the air, your name and mutes in the chat, any invitations you opened, and the key of any station you started here (never its 24 words). The key and invitations are locked under a key the browser keeps to itself. On this page it also keeps the schedules of all of HOSTIS’s stations alike, so the next visit starts sooner. None of it is sent anywhere.
